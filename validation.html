Research preview: 27 October 2026

Synthetic evaluation only. Independent source review has been deferred. The safety corrections and offline ML comparators are available for research; they are not approved for patient care.

  • Human source approvals: 0 of 24. This includes 11 clinical/safety files and 13 software/ML/release files. AI-generated review entries are not human approvals.
  • The corrected engine passed 1,056 tests and 122 subtests. The updated review tool passed 26 separate Git-dependent checks. Software checks are separate from clinical validation.
  • The deterministic engine, logistic regression, gradient-boosted trees and a small neural network ran on 300 generated trajectories (207 training, 93 test), with no patient or exact-feature overlap between partitions.
  • All four systems solve this small synthetic task easily. These scores do not measure interpretation of real reports, image analysis, calibrated patient risk or benefit to patients.
  • Known development-report disagreements remain: 37 extra and 116 missed obligations under developer-authored labels. Independent adjudication is pending.

The public pages use a synthetic snapshot. The ML comparators run offline; no fitted model is activated for patient care, no hospital connection is enabled and no real patient upload is authorized.
